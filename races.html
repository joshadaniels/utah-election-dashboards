How to read this: “Margin %” is the leader’s lead over the runner-up as a share of the top-two vote. “Margin at first report” is the same figure in the Nov 4 noon release; “Δ margin” is the change since then in percentage points (negative = the race tightened). “Flipped” means the leading candidate’s party differs from the party of the incumbent who held the seat. “Could still flip” means the trailing candidate would need under 60% of the district’s estimated outstanding ballots to overtake (district share of the statewide yet-to-report total, same method as the tracker’s CD4 estimate) — i.e. a plausible, not just mathematical, path on ballots left to count. “Too close” is a margin under 2%.
