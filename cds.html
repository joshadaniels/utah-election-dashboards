By congressional district

Est. left is built per county, the same method as the legislative districts: each CD’s expected total in a county is a blended share of that county’s estimated received (see the Closest Legislative Races page for the formula), minus what it has counted there — then the CDs in each county are scaled so they sum exactly to that county’s remaining pool. CDs use the registration share only (100% current): the 2025 congressional redraw changed CD boundaries, so 2022/2024 CD shares are for different districts and are not comparable — so the CD low–high range collapses to the point. “Est. received” is ballots in results + est. left (no county report exists at CD level). “Registered” is active registered voters as of Nov 3.

How to read this: same definitions as the Closest Legislative Races page. “Could still flip” means the trailing candidate’s needed share of the district’s estimated outstanding ballots is within a plausible range — estimated from the district’s partisan registration (active registered voters, Tableau voter file) plus how much that party’s vote over- or underperformed its registration in the congressional race in that county, with the range from how much that over/underperformance varied across the CD’s counties, and a county-weighted counted-share check (see the Closest Legislative Races page for the full method). “Expected to flip” means the expected share alone is enough. “Too close” is a margin under 2%. “Flipped” compares the leader’s party to the party that held that district number before the 2025 congressional redraw (Moore CD1, Maloy CD2, Kennedy CD3, Owens CD4) — the redraw moved the incumbents, so this is a district-number comparison, not same-boundaries.
