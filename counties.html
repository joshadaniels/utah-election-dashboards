Est. left = county’s estimated total ballots received − ballots in results. Each county’s estimate comes from that county’s own Ballot Statistics report posted on the state results site (24 of 29 counties, read Nov 6; the as-of time under each figure is the county report’s own timestamp). Hard county numbers are marked *; the 5 counties without a usable report (Daggett, Morgan, Piute, San Juan, Wasatch) are estimated at the median yet-to-report ratio of the 24 hard counties (2.23% of reported) and marked ~. “Registered” is active registered voters as of Nov 3 (state voted file). Ballots in results = max ballots-cast across contests in the state report. County reports are snapshots (some from Nov 3–4), so a stale estimate can understate what’s left if more mail arrived since.
