Est. left = county’s estimated total ballots received − ballots in results. Each county’s estimate comes from that county’s own Ballot Statistics report posted on the state results site (26 of 29 counties, read Nov 6; the as-of time under each figure is the county report’s own timestamp). Hard county numbers are marked *; the 3 counties without a usable report (Daggett, Morgan, San Juan) are estimated at the median yet-to-report ratio of the 21 hard counties still counting (2.12% of reported) and marked ~. Five counties (Cache, Emery, Kane, Millard, Piute) completed their official canvass on Nov 10 — their counts are final and their yet-to-report is 0; the received-minus-counted remainder in each is rejected ballots. “Registered” is active registered voters as of Nov 3 (state voted file). Ballots in results = max ballots-cast across contests in the state report. County reports are snapshots (some from Nov 3–4), so a stale estimate can understate what’s left if more mail arrived since.
Likely to count (est.) = est. left − the county Summary Results PDF header gap (ballots the county has already counted but the state results file hasn’t published yet) − ballots expected to be rejected (the county’s own rejected figures where available — Salt Lake County 5,502, Washington County 1,939; otherwise the canvass-derived rejection rate of 1.51% across the 17 parsed canvass counties), floored at 0. Canvassed counties are 0. The point figure has no late-cure allowance — a county at 0 may still see a few late cures before the Nov 23 canvass. Statewide likely to count: 327 of 13,739 yet to report.
