For each congressional district: who is registered there (by party) vs how the district actually voted. Updated as of Tue Nov 17, 2026, 1:46 PM MST (state results) · checked Tue Nov 17, 2026, 2:12 PM MST · registration from the Nov 3 snapshot; vote choice from the latest All-Results release.
How to read this page: each district has two charts side by side. On the left, the registration barometer: one bar per party, bar height = that party’s registered voters in the district, bar fully filled with the party color, labeled with the party’s share of the district’s registered voters. On the right, the vote-share pie: slices sized by raw votes cast for each candidate in that district’s U.S. House race. The table under them compares the two directly — registration share vs vote share — so the gap shows where unaffiliated voters broke and any crossover went. The statewide section at the top pairs two barometers instead: registration on the left, and the summed partisan vote choice across all four U.S. House races on the right, in the same chart form so the two compare directly. This is a registration-mix vs vote-mix comparison, not a turnout measure. It does not say how many registered Republicans, Democrats, or unaffiliated voters actually voted. The Nov 3 “voted” columns are not used anywhere on this page.

Sources & method. Party registration: Ali Majid November 2026 Dashboard (Tableau Public), packaged workbook data extract (agg_test.csv), state voter-file data, Voter Status = Active. As-of: Dashboard published Nov 2, 2026; extract downloaded Nov 3, 2026 (Voter Status = Active).. Method: tableau-direct. Reconciliation: the four Tableau CD active totals sum to 1,784,241 vs the state county active registered total of 1,784,255 (difference -14). Sum of the four Tableau CD active totals vs the state county active registered total. The dashboard extract statewide ACTIVE total is 1,784,243 (includes 2 voters in the CD0 unknown-district bucket, excluded here); the remaining difference is rounding. Tableau CD totals differ from the derived CD totals in district-registration.json (CD1 -18,420; CD2 -23; CD3 -11,432; CD4 +29,454) because the derived totals were estimated from counted ballots by county, while these come directly from the voter file. Source status: The Tableau CD party figures above are now in use as the direct source. The county-apportioned state-data version (Nov 3 county voted file, apportioned by CD county shares) is retained as a fallback — see build_cd_party_registration.py, which writes cd-party-registration-apportioned.json. Vote choice: the state’s All-Results reports (electionresults.utah.gov), latest release Tue Nov 17, 2026, 1:46 PM MST; vote shares are of total candidate votes in each race (over/under votes excluded). The party-voted counts from Nov 3 and the CD race vote totals are different snapshots and do not sum to each other — registration is Nov 3, vote choice is the latest release. No L2 or other third-party registration data was used anywhere on this page. Minor parties (Independent American, Libertarian, Constitution, Forward, Green) are grouped into “Other” in the charts and the comparison table, and broken out in the registration data. All figures are unofficial until canvassed.
