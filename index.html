Utah Election Results Dashboards

Unofficial tracking dashboards built from Utah’s official election results. Updated as of Thu Nov 12, 2026, 11:32 AM MST (state results) · checked Thu Nov 12, 2026, 12:11 PM MST.

Utah 2026 General — Ballot Counting Progress

Ballots counted in the official results report — statewide total, top 5 counties, CD4, SD11, and HD61 — with a column for each results release and an estimate of ballots yet to report.

All Counties — Ballots & Outstanding Estimates

All 29 Utah counties, sortable: ballots in results, change since the last release, estimated ballots left to report, and turnout. Expand any county to see its count at only the releases where it changed.

Closest Legislative Races

Closest Utah State House & Senate races, sortable and filterable: leader and margin now vs the first report, margin change over updates, and flags for party flips, leader changes, and races that could still flip on outstanding ballots.

Congressional Districts

Utah’s four congressional districts: a statewide summary, a per-district table (registered voters, ballots in results, estimated ballots left to report, turnout), and the four U.S. House races with margins and could-still-flip flags.

Party & Vote Choice by Congressional District

For each congressional district: who is registered there (Republican / Democratic / Unaffiliated / Other) vs how the district actually voted — registration barometers side by side with vote-share pies, and a table comparing each party’s registration share with its candidate’s vote share.

District Stats

Every State House, State Senate, and U.S. House district in one sortable table: registered voters by party, the current R-vs-D vote split, and GOP vote strength in 2024.

Data source: Utah Lieutenant Governor’s election results site (electionresults.utah.gov) and county clerk reconciliation pages. These dashboards are unofficial and not affiliated with the State of Utah or any county. Results are unofficial until canvassed.
