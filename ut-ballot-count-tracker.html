Hard county numbers are marked *: Salt Lake County 394,882 est. received − 289,921 reported = 104,961 yet to report (county report as of Nov 4, 5:15pm); Utah County 196,500 est. received − 135,126 reported = 61,374 yet to report (Nov 5). The 5 counties without a usable report (Daggett, Morgan, Piute, San Juan, Wasatch) are estimated at the median yet-to-report ratio of the 24 hard counties (2.24% of reported) — together they contribute only ~485 to the statewide total. CD4's estimate is its share (23.2%) of the statewide total; SD11's is its share of Davis County's remaining pool; HD61's is its share of Utah County's. Note the county reports are snapshots (some from Nov 3–4), so a stale estimate can understate what's left if more mail arrived since — and the "yet to report" pool includes provisional and curable ballots, some of which will not ultimately count. For reference, the already-in-hand backlogs are smaller: Utah County verified-but-not-counted 54,367, Salt Lake County in-process 127,794 — the gap between those and the "yet to report" figures is ballots the counties estimate are still arriving by mail.
