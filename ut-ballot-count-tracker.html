Est. left — how it's figured (yet to be reported = county's estimated total received − ballots in state results): Each county's estimated total received now comes from that county's own Ballot Statistics report posted on the state results site (26 of 29 counties — see the All Counties page for each county's figure and as-of time; the reports are re-checked hourly and the estimates update when a county posts a new report). Hard county numbers are marked *: Salt Lake County 396,269 est. received − 375,666 reported = 20,603 yet to report (county report as of 2026-11-12); Utah County 197,657 est. received − 191,161 reported = 6,496 yet to report (county report as of Nov 10, 9:34pm). Five counties (Cache, Emery, Kane, Millard, Piute) completed their official canvass on Nov 10 — their counts are final and they contribute 0 to the yet-to-report total; the received-minus-counted remainder in each is rejected ballots, not outstanding votes. The 3 counties without a usable report (Daggett, Morgan, San Juan) are estimated at the median yet-to-report ratio of the 21 hard counties still counting (1.53% of reported) — together they contribute only ~131 to the statewide total. CD4, SD11 and HD61 are built per county, not apportioned statewide: each district's expected total in a county is a blended share of that county's estimated received (30% the district's share of the county's active registered voters, 50% its share of the county's 2022 ballots, 20% its share of the county's 2024 ballots; Senate districts not up in 2024 use 40/60 current/2022), minus what it has counted there — then the districts in each county are scaled so they sum exactly to that county's remaining pool. The parenthetical range is the low–high from the spread of those components (a year's share is dropped if that year's race was unopposed, and CDs use the registration share only — the 2025 congressional redraw makes 2022/2024 CD shares non-comparable). See back-data-method.md for the full method. Note the county reports are snapshots (some from Nov 3–4), so a stale estimate can understate what's left if more mail arrived since — and the "yet to report" pool includes provisional and curable ballots, some of which will not ultimately count. For reference, the already-in-hand backlogs are smaller: Utah County verified-but-not-counted 2,180, Salt Lake County in-process 15,848 — the gap between those and the "yet to report" figures is ballots the counties estimate are still arriving by mail.
