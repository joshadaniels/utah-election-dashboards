Est. left — how it's figured (yet to be reported = county's estimated total received − ballots in state results): Each county's estimated total received now comes from that county's own Ballot Statistics report posted on the state results site (24 of 29 counties — see the All Counties page for each county's figure and as-of time; the reports are re-checked hourly and the estimates update when a county posts a new report). Hard county numbers are marked *: Salt Lake County 394,882 est. received − 289,921 reported = 104,961 yet to report (county report as of Nov 4, 5:15pm); Utah County 196,500 est. received − 135,126 reported = 61,374 yet to report (county report as of Nov 5). The 5 counties without a usable report (Daggett, Morgan, Piute, San Juan, Wasatch) are estimated at the median yet-to-report ratio of the 24 hard counties (2.23% of reported) — together they contribute only ~184 to the statewide total. CD4, SD11 and HD61 are built per county, not apportioned statewide: each district's expected total in a county is a blended share of that county's estimated received (30% the district's share of the county's active registered voters, 50% its share of the county's 2022 ballots, 20% its share of the county's 2024 ballots; Senate districts not up in 2024 use 40/60 current/2022), minus what it has counted there — then the districts in each county are scaled so they sum exactly to that county's remaining pool. The parenthetical range is the low–high from the spread of those components (a year's share is dropped if that year's race was unopposed, and CDs use the registration share only — the 2025 congressional redraw makes 2022/2024 CD shares non-comparable). See back-data-method.md for the full method. Note the county reports are snapshots (some from Nov 3–4), so a stale estimate can understate what's left if more mail arrived since — and the "yet to report" pool includes provisional and curable ballots, some of which will not ultimately count. For reference, the already-in-hand backlogs are smaller: Utah County verified-but-not-counted 54,367, Salt Lake County in-process 127,794 — the gap between those and the "yet to report" figures is ballots the counties estimate are still arriving by mail.
