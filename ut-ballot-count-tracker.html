Hard county numbers are marked *: Salt Lake County 397,413 est. received − 390,832 reported = 0 yet to report (county report as of Nov 17, 5:34pm (canvass)); Utah County 197,667 est. received − 194,737 reported = 0 yet to report (county report as of Nov 17, 2:16pm (canvass)). 28 counties (Beaver, Box Elder, Cache, Carbon, Daggett, Davis, Duchesne, Emery, Garfield, Grand, Iron, Juab, Kane, Millard, Morgan, Piute, Rich, Salt Lake, Sanpete, Sevier, Summit, Tooele, Uintah, Utah, Wasatch, Washington, Wayne, Weber) have completed their official canvass — their counts are final and they contribute 0 to the yet-to-report total; the received-minus-counted remainder in each is rejected ballots (rejected provisionals, uncured curable ballots, and non-curable rejections), not outstanding votes. The 1 counties without a usable report (San Juan) are estimated at the median yet-to-report ratio of the 0 hard counties still counting (3.62% of reported) — together they contribute only ~187 to the statewide total. CD4, SD11 and HD61 are built per county, not apportioned statewide: each district's expected total in a county is a blended share of that county's estimated received (30% the district's share of the county's active registered voters, 50% its share of the county's 2022 ballots, 20% its share of the county's 2024 ballots; Senate districts not up in 2024 use 40/60 current/2022), minus what it has counted there — then the districts in each county are scaled so they sum exactly to that county's remaining pool. The parenthetical range is the low–high from the spread of those components (a year's share is dropped if that year's contest was unopposed, and CDs use the registration share only — the 2025 congressional redraw makes 2022/2024 CD shares non-comparable). See back-data-method.md for the full method. Note the county reports are snapshots (some from Nov 3–4), so a stale estimate can understate what's left if more mail arrived since — and the "yet to report" pool includes provisional and curable ballots, some of which will not ultimately count. For reference, the already-in-hand backlogs are smaller: Utah County verified-but-not-counted 0, Salt Lake County in-process 5,578 — the gap between those and the "yet to report" figures is ballots the counties estimate are still arriving by mail.
Likely to count (est.) — the yet-to-report pool minus ballots the county has already counted but the state results file hasn't published yet (the county Summary Results PDF "Ballots Cast" header runs ahead of the contest feed) and minus ballots expected to be rejected (the county's own rejected figures where available — Salt Lake County 5,502, Washington County 1,939; otherwise the canvass-derived rejection rate of 1.67% across the 27 parsed canvass counties). Canvassed counties are 0. The point figure is max(0, …) with no late-cure allowance — a county at 0 may still see a few late cures before the Nov 23 canvass.
